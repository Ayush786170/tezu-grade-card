How to Use

Follow these simple steps to view your grade card.

  1. 1

    Enter your roll number.

    Use your university-issued Roll Number or Enrollment ID.

  2. 2

    Select “View Grade Card”.

    Click the button to fetch your grade card.

  3. 3

    Review your grade card.

    Your grade card opens in a new tab (PDF).

  4. 4

    Download if needed.

    Save or print for your records.
